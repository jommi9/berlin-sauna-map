        prices change

        This is a personal, non-commercial guide with no affiliation to any venue listed. Prices and opening hours, opening hours and Urban Sports Club terms are recorded as each venue publishes them and change without notice, so check with the venue before a special trip. Where a schedule could not be confirmed, the card says so rather than guessing.

        where the data comes from

        Every figure is read off the venue’s own site and re-checked weekly; corrections are welcome. Locations from OpenStreetMap; roads, rail, water, parks and district outlines © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

        the panel

        Reviews come from a small panel rather than the open internet, so a review means something. Anyone who uses Helsinki’s saunas can ask to join.
