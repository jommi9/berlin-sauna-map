    Before you go

    For a normal sauna session rather than a luxury spa day, KIEZ SAUNA, Saunabad, Olivin and Lützow are better benchmarks than most hotel spas. Hotel spas make sense when the pool, terrace, relaxation area, or USC access is part of what you want.

    Two traps

    Liquidrom is on Urban Sports Club, but USC entry does not include the sauna — you still pay the supplement. Stadtbad Neukölln’s sauna is on summer break through 31 October 2026.
