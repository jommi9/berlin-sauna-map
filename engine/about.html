about

the guide

A personal, non-commercial guide to the saunas of __CITY__, with no affiliation to any venue listed.

the data

Every price and opening hour__USC_TERMS__ is read off the venue’s own site and re-checked weekly, most recently on __LAST_SCAN__. Venues change them without notice, so check with the venue before a special trip. Where a schedule could not be confirmed, the card says so.

Corrections are welcome on GitHub.

__NOTES__
listings

Listings are added by contributors (__CONTRIBUTORS__), each one checked against the venue’s own site and merged by the editor. Add a sauna or open a new city; from anyone else the same form is a suggestion.

reviews

A small panel writes the reviews. Anyone who goes to these saunas can ask to join from “the panel” at the foot of the map.
