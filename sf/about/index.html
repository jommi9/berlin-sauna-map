about

the guide

A personal, non-commercial guide to the saunas of San Francisco, with no affiliation to any venue listed.

the data

Every price and opening hour is read off the venue’s own site and re-checked weekly, most recently on 25.11.2026. Venues change them without notice, so check with the venue before a special trip. Where a schedule could not be confirmed, the card says so.

Corrections are welcome on GitHub.

listings

Listings are added by contributors (@jommi9, @Jaamuru), each one checked against the venue’s own site and merged by the editor. Add a sauna or open a new city; from anyone else the same form is a suggestion.

reviews

A small panel writes the reviews. Anyone who goes to these saunas can ask to join from “the panel” at the foot of the map.
